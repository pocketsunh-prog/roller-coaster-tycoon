How to play

  1. Build track pieces from the end of the station (ghost preview).
  2. Loop the track back to the station entrance to complete the circuit.
  3. Press Open Ride — guests will pay to ride!
  • 1-6 place piece   Q/E cycle piece   Enter place selected
  • Click a piece to select it   1-6 replace   Del remove   Esc deselect
  • U undo   C clear   O open/close ride   -/+ ticket price
  • Z zoom in   X zoom out   V save   L load   G gift shop
  • Build a gift shop ($500) — riders stop for souvenirs after their ride.
  • Mouse drag = rotate, wheel = zoom, right-drag = pan, click ghost = build
  • Up slopes have chain lifts. Higher ticket price = fewer guests willing to pay.
  • Pick a coaster model (Steel / Wooden / Hyper) — each has its own look and speed.
